# Source Note: Quantreo Meta-Labelling Article

- Source: https://www.newsletter.quantreo.com/p/meta-labelling-explained-filter-noise
- Title: `Meta-Labelling Explained: Filter Noise, Boost Precision, Win More`
- Role in this experiment: practical explanation of a primary-signal plus secondary-filter workflow.

## Relevant Takeaways

- A primary model can identify potential opportunities while a meta model filters which opportunities are worth taking.
- The practical objective is higher precision, not more trades.
- Triple-barrier style labels can be used to train the second layer on trade outcomes.
