# Trading Breakouts with Order Flow Confirmation

• By OrderflowHQ Team

![Trading Breakouts with Order Flow Confirmation](/images/breakouts.svg)

Breakouts are powerful trading opportunities, but most fail. Order flow helps you identify the real breakouts from the false ones, dramatically improving your success rate.

## Why Most Breakouts Fail

### The Problem

Traditional breakout trading:

* **No confirmation**: Just price movement
* **False breakouts**: Most don’t follow through
* **Late entries**: Miss the move
* **Poor exits**: Don’t know when to exit

### The Solution

Order flow confirmation:

* **Volume confirmation**: Real interest
* **Delta confirmation**: Buying/selling pressure
* **Depth confirmation**: Orders behind move
* **True breakouts**: High probability

## Identifying Real Breakouts with Order Flow

### High Volume Breakouts

Real breakouts have high volume:

**Signs:**

* Volume increasing on breakout
* Volume above average
* Strong buying/selling pressure
* Sustained volume

**Trading:**

* Enter on breakout
* Confirm with volume
* Use stops below/above level

### Delta Confirmation

Real breakouts have strong delta:

**Signs:**

* Positive delta on bullish breakout
* Negative delta on bearish breakout
* Strong buying/selling pressure
* Sustained delta

**Trading:**

* Enter on breakout
* Confirm with delta
* Use stops below/above level

### Market Depth Confirmation

Real breakouts have orders behind them:

**Signs:**

* Large orders at breakout level
* Orders disappearing (filled)
* New orders appearing
* Strong momentum

**Trading:**

* Enter on breakout
* Confirm with depth
* Use stops below/above level

## Avoiding False Breakouts

### False Breakout Signs

Watch for these warning signs:

**Low Volume:**

* Breakout with low volume
* No real interest
* Likely to fail
* False signal

**Weak Delta:**

* Breakout with weak delta
* No buying/selling pressure
* Likely to fail
* False signal

**No Orders:**

* Breakout with no orders
* No depth behind move
* Likely to fail
* False signal

### Real Breakout Signs

Look for these confirmation signals:

**High Volume:**

* Breakout with high volume
* Real interest
* Likely to succeed
* True signal

**Strong Delta:**

* Breakout with strong delta
* Strong buying/selling pressure
* Likely to succeed
* True signal

**Orders Behind:**

* Breakout with orders
* Depth behind move
* Likely to succeed
* True signal
