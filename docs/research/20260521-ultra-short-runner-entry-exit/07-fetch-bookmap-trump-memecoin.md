## **The $Trump Trade: A Fast-Moving Boom and Bust**

![](https://blog.bookmap.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2_result-6.webp)

![](https://blog.bookmap.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2_result-6.webp)

On January 17, 2025, just minutes after Donald Trump announced the launch of the $Trump cryptocurrency, an unidentified trader made a $1,096,109 bet on the token at its opening price of 18 cents (Reference: [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/02/09/us/politics/trump-crypto-memecoin.html)). Within two days, the price had surged to $75 per token, allowing early buyers to cash out with massive profits—some making as much as $109 million.

However, the boom was short-lived. The price eventually crashed, wiping out more than $2 billion in cumulative losses for latecomers. More than 810,000 wallets ended up underwater. Meanwhile, the Trump family and its partners reportedly collected nearly $100 million in trading fees from the frenzy.

This cycle—whales and insiders making massive early profits while retail traders suffer losses—is a pattern that repeats in speculative assets, especially memecoins. Traders need to recognize the signals of early whale accumulation and liquidity traps to protect their investments.
