# Requirements

User-approved goal-process change:

- Persist the rule in the goal project that each goal round must follow the full business workflow.
- One full business round maps to one active CCG task.
- Do not archive the CCG task after a single intermediate step such as a question, live status check, attribution note, probe, report, or partial experiment.
- Archive only after the full business round reaches a clear business decision and loop closure.
- The rule must not force standalone user questions or health-only status passes to become business-round tasks.
- Keep the wording aligned with the existing "Complete Optimization Round" terminology.

Scope:

- Edit only the goal/process documentation needed to enforce this rule.
- Do not change runtime code, model config, or live trading behavior.
